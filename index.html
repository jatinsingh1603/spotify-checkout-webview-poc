What this demonstrates
SpotifyCheckoutCallbackActivity (exported, BROWSABLE) accepts spotify-checkout://payment-callback/<uuid>?status=success&success_url=<URL>, URL-decodes success_url and loads it in the checkout WebView with no origin, scheme or allow-list validation. Any web page or installed app can supply that URL.
